# Virat CRM - User Guide

Welcome to the Virat CRM. This guide covers the essential features for Field Agents, Managers, and Admins.

## 1. Getting Started

- **Login**: Use your Employee Code and Password to sign in.
- **Profile**: Visit the **Profile** page to update your details and manage notification settings.

## 2. Real-time Notifications

The CRM supports native browser notifications to alert you about status updates (e.g., Sale Approved, Leave Rejected).

- **How to Enable**:
  1. Go to your **Profile**.
  2. Locate the **Web Push Notifications** card.
  3. Click **Enable Notifications**.
  4. Allow the permission prompt in your browser.
- **Note**: Notifications work even if the browser tab is closed (on supported devices).

## 3. Offline Operations (Field Agents)

Field work often takes you to areas with poor connectivity. The CRM handles this gracefully.

- **Logging Sales/Replacements**: You can submit forms even when offline.
- **Local Queue**: If you are offline, the CRM will save your submission locally on your device.
- **Syncing**: Once you regain internet access, the app will automatically detect the connection and upload your pending items in the background.
- **Indicators**: Look at the top of your dashboard for status bars like:
  - `Offline: 2 Pending Sync` (Items saved on your device)
  - `Syncing 2 Items...` (Uploading in progress)

## 4. Analytics & Reporting (Managers/Admins)

- **Dashboard**: View real-time charts and metrics for your team's performance.
- **Exports**: Use the **Export CSV** button in the Analytics section to download raw data for external reporting.

## 5. Leave Management

- **Apply**: Use the **Leaves** section to request time off.
- **Approvals**: Managers will receive a notification and can approve or reject requests directly from their dashboard.
